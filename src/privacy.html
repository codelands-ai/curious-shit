Curious Shit

Privacy

You can scroll and search this gallery without an account. Nothing about you is stored for that.

What we store

When you react to a poster, we store the reaction, the time, and your Firebase user ID: a random string that identifies your account but not you. We don't store your name, your email address or anything else. Removing a reaction deletes it.

Signing in

Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service. Firebase holds your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google, under Google's privacy policy. This site never receives or keeps them.

Firebase keeps you signed in using your browser's storage. If you ask for an email sign-in link, your address is also kept in this browser until you open the link, so you don't have to type it again.

Who else sees your visit

The site is served by Cloudflare, and its fonts come from Google Fonts. Like any web request, both see your IP address and browser details. We use no analytics, no ads and no tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

You can remove any reaction by tapping it again. To delete all your reactions and your sign-in account, email willt@codelands.ai.
